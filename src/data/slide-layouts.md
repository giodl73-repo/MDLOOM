# proof Slide Layouts

| layout | directive | zones | use-case |
|--------|-----------|-------|----------|
| title | layout=title | title, subtitle, author, date | Opening slide |
| title-content | layout=title-content | title, body | Standard content slide |
| two-column | layout=two-column | title, left-body, right-body | Comparison or side-by-side |
| section | layout=section | title, subtitle | Chapter divider |
| stats | layout=stats | title, stats-body | KPI / numbers slide |
| blank | layout=blank | body | Free-form canvas |
