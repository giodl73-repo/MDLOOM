# Writing Style Guide — Block Quotes

Attributed quotations in technical writing provide grounding and authority.

## Classic Attribution

<!-- proof:compiled from="proof:blockquote" -->
> Premature optimization is the root of all evil.
>
> — Donald Knuth
<!-- /proof:compiled -->

## Multi-line with Double Border

<!-- proof:compiled from="proof:blockquote" -->
> Adding manpower to a late software project makes it later.
>
> — Fred Brooks, The Mythical Man-Month
<!-- /proof:compiled -->

## Indented Style (No Bar)

<!-- proof:compiled from="proof:blockquote" -->
> The best way to predict the future is to invent it.
>
> — Alan Kay
<!-- /proof:compiled -->

## Usage Notes

Block quotes work best when the attribution carries weight. Use sparingly —
one or two per guide is usually right.
